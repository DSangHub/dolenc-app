Why fees sit on top

If someone chooses $50, the family gets $50. Dolenc 2% and Stripe are added to the card charge. Same rule for the page goal: a $400 need is listed as $400 plus fees = total needed.

How Dolenc works

The family stays in control. Friends do not need an account to read the page. They use the hashtag you send them.

  1. Someone close to the event creates a short profile: who or what was lost, what happened, optional photo, zip code, and the dollar amount they need.
  2. Dolenc issues a unique hashtag and shows family need + fees = total needed.
  3. You tell relatives and friends yourself — text, email, or a printed note. Nothing is blasted from the app.
  4. They open the hashtag page and leave words, a prayer, and optionally $15–$1,000. Fees are added to what they pay.
  5. Optional paid geo flyer: announce the page by zip so nearby people who want to help can find it.

Death & funeral

Accident or illness

“Hoping for a speedy recovery.” Help with bills, rides, or time off work.

Pet tragedy

A small circle, a sincere note, help with unexpected vet costs.

Steps against fake claims

Document when money is involved

Pages that accept currency may be asked for a death notice, hospital or vet receipt, or a police report number before payouts release.

Invite is off-platform

You choose who gets the hashtag. Dolenc does not scrape contacts or post to social feeds.

Hold & review

First payouts can sit in a short review window. Reports of fraud freeze the page.

Dolenc is not a charity, insurer, or funeral director. It is a messaging and payment rail. Families remain responsible for how gifts are used.

For funeral homes and card makers

Be findable when a family in your zip is already gathering condolences. No annual contract required for the waitlist.

Funeral home profile

Hours, service styles, a respectful flyer tied to nearby hashtags. Families opt in before you appear on their page.

Card sellers

Print or digital sympathy cards. Buyer pays you through Dolenc. We take 8% for the placement.

Geo flyer

Paid, zip-bounded announcement. Not a social blast. Meant for neighbors who already want to help.
