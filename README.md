# Dolenc.app

Condolences when you cannot be there. A family opens a loss page, gets a hashtag, and invites people by text or email. Friends reply with words and optionally a $15–$1,000 gift.

Live file: [index.html](index.html)

## Fees added on top

The amount the family names is the amount they receive. Dolenc 2% and Stripe (est. 2.9% + $0.30) are added to the payer total and shown as **total needed**.

Example: $50 to the family → about $52.78 charged.

## Partner fees

- Card sellers: 8% of introduced sales
- Funeral homes / geo flyers: 8% of processed flyer or referred checkout

## Trust

Pages that accept money may require a death notice, receipt, or police report number before payout.
